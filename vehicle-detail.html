The verdict

Brilliant to drive. Demanding to live with.

Pros

  • Exceptional acceleration and handling
  • Class-leading range (320 miles EPA est.)
  • Access to reliable fast-charging network
  • Over-the-air software updates

Cons

  • Firm ride quality over rough pavement
  • Over-reliance on touchscreen controls
  • Rear visibility is compromised

Who it's for

Tech enthusiasts and commuters wanting zero emissions without sacrificing performance.

Who should skip

Buyers looking for plush, cloud-like ride comfort or physical buttons for climate control.

FAQ

Before you write in.

Can I request a vehicle review?

Absolutely. Send us the make and model and we'll add it to our testing queue. Popular requests are prioritized.

How do I submit an owner review?

Owner reviews require proof of purchase so we can mark them as verified. Reach out via the form and we'll send instructions.

I found an error in a spec. What now?

Thank you! Let us know the page and the correct figure with a source, and our editorial team will verify and update it.

Do you accept sponsored reviews?

No. We never accept payment for coverage or scores. Partnership enquiries are limited to clearly-marked affiliate pricing links.

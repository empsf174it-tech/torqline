How we score

Reading the matrix.

What does the editorial score measure?

It's a weighted average of our 10-point test protocol: performance, efficiency, comfort, tech & safety, and value. Each criterion is scored on the road, not from a spec sheet.

Why are some values highlighted in red?

The highlight marks the class-leading figure in that row — fastest 0–60, lowest price, highest score — so the strongest option jumps out at a glance.

What does "Show differences only" hide?

Any row where every vehicle shares the same value. It's the fastest way to see what actually separates your shortlist.

Do affiliate links affect the ranking?

No. Scores are locked by the editorial team before any commercial partner is involved. Affiliate commissions fund testing, never results.
